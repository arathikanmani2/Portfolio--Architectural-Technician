Project Overview

Sandbank Point is a development of 14 zero-carbon townhouses, currently progressing through installation. The project involves complex material build-ups, where coordinating the interfaces between layers is essential to maintaining aligned external finishes and achieving the architect's design intent.

I developed detailed Revit models for each block and used live sections to review material interfaces, coordinate build-ups and identify potential clashes before installation. This helped resolve alignment issues and improve the consistency of the coordinated design information.

During the initial days of installation, the coordinated elements fitted together on site without clashes. The development has also attracted local attention, including visits from Wirral Council representatives and local councillors.
