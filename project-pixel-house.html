House

Residential Architecture · Alappuzha, Kerala, India

A residential project exploring massing, spatial organisation, climatic response and architectural character within an existing residential site.

PROJECT TYPE Residential
AREA 3,244 sq. ft.
LOCATION Alappuzha, Kerala
SOFTWARE AutoCAD · SketchUp · Revit · Lumion

PROJECT

Design development

This residential project of 3,244 sq. ft. in Alappuzha, Kerala, India, was developed on a site containing an existing residential building. The design responds to the client's requirements while carefully considering functionality, spatial organisation and architectural character.

The house incorporates living spaces, an integrated gourmet kitchen, four suites, a gym, home theatre and swimming pool. Particular attention was given to mass distribution, circulation, privacy, climatic considerations and the relationship between the new architecture and the existing site conditions.
