PRODUCT & DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

Bespoke Chimney
Assembly

Developing a bespoke volumetric chimney assembly through alternative façade finishes, architectural coordination and design for manufacture and installation.

Project type Residential
Project focus Design Development & DfMA
Software Revit · AutoCAD · Rendering

Bespoke chimney assembly developed as part of the building's external architectural envelope.

01 / ARCHITECTURAL INTENT

Understanding the architect's vision.

Architectural visualisation illustrating the intended appearance of the building and chimney.

The architectural visualisation establishes the intended appearance of the building and the role of the chimney within the overall composition.

This provided the architectural context for developing a bespoke assembly that responds to the intended form and material character while considering the practical requirements of manufacture and installation.

02 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

From architectural intent to a buildable assembly.

The chimney was developed as a bespoke architectural element intended to complement the surrounding built context while accommodating the practical requirements of off-site manufacture and site installation.

Two external finish options were explored: brick cladding and standing-seam metal. These alternatives provided different interpretations of the same architectural element.

The design development considered the chimney as a coordinated assembly, bringing together its external appearance, constituent components and interfaces with the wider building envelope.

03 / DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

Exploring two façade options.

Alternative finishes were developed to explore how the chimney could respond to the building's architectural character while retaining a consistent assembly concept.

OPTION 01

Brick-clad finish

A masonry-inspired external finish intended to relate the chimney to the surrounding buildings.

OPTION 02

Standing-seam finish

An alternative metal-clad treatment exploring a different material expression and façade finish.

04 / ASSEMBLY BREAKDOWN

Understanding the components.

The assembly was considered in terms of its constituent elements and their relationship with the supporting structure and adjoining building components.

The exploded view communicates the assembly concept and helps explain how the individual elements contribute to the overall architectural form.

This stage connected the external design intent with the practical considerations of assembly, coordination and buildability.

05 / FACTORY MANUFACTURE

From design to factory production.

Developing the chimney as a factory-made assembly required consideration of how the design could be translated into a practical component for transportation and installation.

The design approach considered material interfaces, assembly requirements and buildability while maintaining the intended external appearance.

06 / SITE INSTALLATION

Designed with installation in mind.

Considering installation alongside design development helped connect the proposed assembly with the practical requirements of site construction.

The approach placed emphasis on the relationship between the prefabricated component, the building structure and the surrounding external envelope.

07 / COMPLETED RESULT

The assembly in context.

08 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Design development and technical coordination.

My contribution focused on developing and communicating alternative chimney design options, considering their material expression and supporting the transition from architectural concept to a coordinated assembly.

  • Developed alternative brick-clad and standing-seam design options.
  • Produced visual material communicating the assembly from multiple viewpoints.
  • Explored the relationship between the external finish and the assembly components.
  • Considered buildability, manufacture and site installation during design development.
