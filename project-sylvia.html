Residential Architecture

A 2,900 sq. ft. residence designed for a high-contour site, combining contemporary architectural form with passive environmental strategies and carefully developed technical information.

PROJECT TYPE Residential
AREA 2,900 sq. ft.
SITE High-contour site
SOFTWARE AutoCAD · SketchUp · Revit · Lumion

PROJECT

Design approach

This 2,900 sq. ft. residence is designed around a high-contour site rising from the road, allowing the building elevation to become a prominent visual element. The contemporary multi-box composition uses a restrained palette of texture and colour, with exterior spotlights used to emphasise key architectural focal points.

The spatial planning focuses on creating generous, well-connected living areas with maximum access to daylight and natural ventilation. Larger windows and openings enhance the quality of the interior environment, while the front elevation incorporates a spacious sit-out and balcony oriented towards views of the Ghats.

Set within a contoured site surrounded by rubber plantations, the design responds to the local climate through passive ventilation strategies and careful placement of openings. The spaces are efficiently organised so that every part of the plan contributes to the overall functionality of the house.
