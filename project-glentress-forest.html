Project Overview

A leisure development of 56 lodges, ranging from one to four bedrooms, set within the woodland of Glentress National Park. The project required careful planning of lodge positioning and installation sequences to minimise disruption to the surrounding forestry.

My Contribution

This project marked the transition from AutoCAD to Revit-based documentation. My work included developing technical junction details, defining wall build-up priorities at internal and external corners, and preparing DfMA packs for wall and floor assemblies.

Particular attention was given to coordinating façade cladding across floor levels to maintain consistent alignment and design quality.
